Other Products & Services

Worthington Federal Savings Bank also offers other products and services to our customers such as:

  • 24 Hour ATM in our drive up at the Worthington location
  • 24 Hour ATM in the entryway at the Jackson location
  • Safe Deposit Boxes
  • Visa Access Card. (formerly called VISA travel cards)
  • VISA Gift Cards
  • Money Orders/Cashiers Checks
  • Notary Public
  • Credit Card available if qualified
  • Wire Transfer (must be conducted in person, signature required)
  • Telephone Transfer (caller must be on both accounts)
  • 24 Hour Online Banking
  • Mobile App
  • Mobile Deposit
  • BillPay

Visit us at any of our three locations
