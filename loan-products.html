HOME LOANS
A home mortgage is one of the largest and most important financial obligations you may ever assume.  At Worthington Federal Savings Bank, we strive to find the mortgage that is right for you, whether it is a fixed or adjustable rate mortgage. 
We do not sell our mortgages to a third party. Therefore, when you have a question, you have a local person to contact. 

HOME EQUITY LOANS
Worthington Federal Savings Bank makes it easy for you to use the equity you have in your home to finance purchases.  We offer Home Equity Loans with a fixed rate for a fixed term or a Line of Credit that you may draw on as your needs change.
Home Equity loan interest payments may be tax deductible.  Consult your tax advisor on the deductibility.

HOME IMPROVEMENT LAONS
For all types of improvements to your home from inside to out, Worthington Federal Savings Bank can assist you in improving the comfort of your home.  We offer a variety of funding options for these products.

INSTALLMENT LOANS
Whether you are buying a new or used vehicle, motorcycle, RV or boat, Worthington Federal Savings Bank offers all types of consumer loans to fit your individual needs.

COMMERCIAL LOANS
Worthington Federal Savings Bank provides loans on commercial and agricultural real estate and equipment.  These loans have a competitive interest rate and the payment schedule can be built to fit your needs.

Please contact us or visit one of our locations for more information on any of the loan products listed above.
