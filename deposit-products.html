DEPOSIT PRODUCTS > CHECKING

NOW ACCOUNT

  • Electronic deposits and withdrawals are allowed
  • BillPay
  • $200.00 minimum balance
  • Competitive interest rate
  • $10.00 monthly fee – waived if minimum balance of $200.00 is maintained, automatic payment on a Worthington Federal loan, or an ACH deposit of a Social Security benefit.
  • Unlimited transactions
  • VISA Debit Card available
  • Statement mailed monthly or you can enroll in electronic statements.
  • Overdraft protection available if qualified.
  • Mobile Deposit

MONEY MARKET DEPOSIT ACCOUNT

  • Electronic deposits and withdrawals are allowed
  • $2,500.00 minimum balance
  • Tiered interest rates
  • $10.00 monthly fee – waived if minimum balance of $2,500.00 is maintained
  • Limited Transactions. Minimum withdrawal is $500.00
  • Limited check writing available - checks must be written for at least $500.00
  • Statement is mailed monthly or you can enroll in electronic statements.
  • Mobile Deposit

SIMPLE CHECKING

  • Electronic deposits and withdrawals are allowed
  • BillPay
  • No minimum balance
  • No monthly fees
  • Unlimited transactions
  • VISA Debit Card available
  • Statement is mailed monthly or you can enroll in electronic statements.
  • Overdraft protection available, if qualified.
  • Non-interest bearing
  • Mobile Deposit
  • $25 to open

BUDGET (SMALL BUSINESS) CHECKING

  • Electronic deposits and withdrawals are allowed
  • BillPay
  • No minimum balance
  • $3.00 monthly fee
  • $0.10 per item charge for each debit item
  • Unlimited transactions
  • VISA Debit Card available
  • Statement is mailed monthly or you can enroll in electronic statements.
  • Overdraft protection available, if qualified.
  • Non-interest bearing
  • Mobile Deposit
