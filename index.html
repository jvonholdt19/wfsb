Rooted since 1935

Founded to help the community prosper and make it possible for people to build homes. The first loan went on the books on March 30, 1935.

Merger complete

Welcome, Jackson Federal customers

Jackson Federal Savings and Loan Association is now part of Worthington Federal Savings Bank. You can now do your banking at any of our three locations: Worthington, Lakefield, and Jackson.

  1. August 27, 2025 — Jackson Federal Savings and Loan Association merged with Worthington Federal Savings Bank.
  2. March 16, 2026 — The complete merger took effect.
