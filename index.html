Hello and welcome to Worthington Federal Savings Bank's Website. We are pleased you have taken your time to visit us and hope you find it both beneficial and informative. In this "Information Age", we understand the importance of you being able to access your account information when you need it, and we are excited about the opportunity to provide you with the convenience of our Internet Banking services.

At Worthington Federal Savings Bank, we strive to offer that banking service our customers expect. Most importantly, our experienced staff delivers these services in a friendly, efficient manner.  We are proud to be a part of our communities, and to participate in the growth and prosperity of our region.

We invite you to visit us at any time, and if you have suggestions as to how we may make this site more useful to you, please communicate those ideas to us.

Since 1935

On March 30th, 1935, the first loan went on the books and was in the amount of $800.00. This was the beginning of our service to Worthington and the surrounding communities.

Welcome, Jackson Federal customers

On August 27, 2025, Jackson Federal Savings and Loan Association merged with Worthington Federal Savings Bank. The complete merger happened on March 16th, 2026, so our customers now have the ability to conduct their banking business at any of our three locations.
